# Owner style preferences for planning and insight decks

These are the owner's review preferences, learned over four review rounds of a planning deck. They apply to every native-route planning or insight deck unless the request or the selected template says otherwise. The template still governs fonts, sizes, logo and layout.

## Colour and surfaces

- Do not use near-black flat fills for cards, bars, nodes or bands. Use the reference "insight" look instead:
  - Header bars: slate gradient (for example `343B45 → 606A77`) or brand-red gradient (`9F0C16 → CC3B43`).
  - Content areas: light vertical gradients (grey `F0F2F5 → FFFFFF`, red tint `FBEDEF → FFFFFF`).
- If diagram nodes still read as black, switch them to silver (`606A77 → 8C96A3`, or light silver with dark text).
- When a page shows owners or roles, colour-code the annotations by business module. Give each module one distinct fill, and use the same colour for that module's icons and lead-ins elsewhere on the page.

## Icons and text

- Every header bar carries a small line icon, and so does every bullet.
- Write bullets as a red bold lead-in followed by a full sentence, with an owner and timing where relevant (`① Lead: sentence · owner · Q2`). Telegraph style is rejected, especially when describing field units.
- Aim for high density with a clear hierarchy (sub-headings in each column). The template's 14pt body floor limits density, so add a build-time guard that estimates line counts per box and fails on overflow or label collisions.

## Bottom key-message band

- Use a light red-tint gradient band with a brand-red pill (icon plus label), dark text, and the key words in red bold.
- Use two labels with two different icons, never one generic label everywhere:
  - Insight pages: 启示 (insight), with a bulb icon.
  - Business design, key initiative and closing pages: 总结 (summary), with a check icon.

## Insight pages

- Cite the source event or conference brief and its page on every insight page.
- When one page cannot carry both, split the insight into two pages:
  1. The abstracted model, drawn natively, with the reading for us.
  2. The original figures (full source pages, any classification marks kept visible) with a short reading of their key points.
- Internal material may be quoted when the owner confirms it is internal. Never crop out classification marks.
- Use the source's numbers, not remembered or dictated figures. Note when a number comes only from a talk ("现场讲述") and not from the slides.

## Planning pages

- Put a top strip with problem / goal / measure. Use a smaller textless visual with anchored native labels.
- The right column is the key initiatives, with a sub-heading and five full sentences.
- The lower left holds the core deliverables and the dependencies.
- Do not list field units or core countries when a schematic is enough.

## Deck frame

- The cover gets a textless generated visual. Cut out its white background so it sits on the template's cover art.
- End with a Thank You page that carries the take-aways (one sentence each, plus the next actions).
- Before writing names and titles, check them against the owner's role vocabulary. A phonetically similar title is a factual error.
