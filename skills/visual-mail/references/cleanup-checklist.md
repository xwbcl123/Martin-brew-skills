# Delivery Checks

- Check that subject, body, links and screenshot match the intended audience and source.
- Remove internal production notes, credentials and private paths. Preserve real content about agents, workers, AI or prompts; keyword matches require contextual review.
- Use the selected brand's Logo, footer and byline. Do not add CSTC/RSPO identity to unrelated content.
- For drafts, unresolved links may remain clearly marked. Resolve them before actual sending, or omit unavailable optional links with an accurate explanation.
- Verify the screenshot against the HTML and confirm the embed resolves within the approved output root.
- For actual email sending, use a delivery-compatible attachment/inline image; Obsidian embeds alone do not prove that recipients receive an image.
- Preserve requested draft labels and confidentiality markings. A final delivery must not claim publication that did not occur.
- Check prior delivery receipts before retries. Record package-ready, sent, and publication states separately.
