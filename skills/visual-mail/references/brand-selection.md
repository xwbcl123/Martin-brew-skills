# Brand Selection

1. Use the user's selected brand or template. Do not select another style.
2. Otherwise use the project `brand-guidelines` Skill to classify Life versus Work.
3. Use the selected identity's colors, fonts, Logo, byline and footer. Do not rotate brands by topic or history.
4. If no applicable identity is available, use a neutral bundled fallback and record the missing brand input. Do not invent a Logo or company affiliation.

Read only the selected brand's tokens. A third-party brand explicitly requested by the user takes precedence over Martin defaults. Template-specific Logo geometry and typography take precedence over generic visual-mail defaults. The sample fallback styles are not mandatory designs.
