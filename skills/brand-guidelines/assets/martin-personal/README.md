# Martin Personal Brand System

## Identity

**Promise**: From complexity to clarity · 化繁为简，洞见本质
**Method**: 由此及彼，由表及里，去粗存精，去伪存真
**Personality**: Calm Authority · Evidence-led Insight · Human Curiosity

The Balanced Signal mark combines a stable capital M with a restrained current cut through its center. The M carries structure and credibility; the current represents movement from surface complexity toward underlying clarity. The artistic `Martin` wordmark adds a human signature without weakening the compact emblem.

- `final/martin-m-emblem-navy.svg` - formal master for light backgrounds and small applications.

| Signal Mint | `#16FFBB` | Secondary digital accent |
| Ice Blue | `#37A7E7` | Supporting data/diagram accent |
| Warm White | `#F5F7F6` | Default editorial canvas |
| Ink | `#172033` | Body text |
| Slate | `#697386` | Secondary text |

Formal artifacts use flat Deep Navy. Gradients are an expressive digital treatment, not the master Logo.

## Usage

- Use this identity only for `Life`: personal insight, learning, reflection, and creative work.
- Do not place it as a co-equal Logo on Organization Brand company work.
- On Work artifacts, credit Martin through a byline or author field when appropriate.
- Minimum emblem size: 16 px digital / 6 mm print. At very small sizes, use the flat emblem.
- Clear space: at least one quarter of the emblem width on every side.
- Do not redraw the signal, add shadows, add 3D effects, rotate the mark, or combine it with corporate symbols.
