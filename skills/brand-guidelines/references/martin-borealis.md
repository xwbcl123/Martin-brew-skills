# Martin-Borealis v2

## Role

Martin-Borealis is the named dark expressive variant of the `Life` personal brand. Use it for personal digital covers, keynotes, story-led essays, creative experiments, and high-impact visual pieces. It is not the default for dense reports and it is not a Work/Organization Brand theme.

## Identity

- Use the Balanced Signal M and artistic `Martin` wordmark from `assets/martin-personal/final/`.
- The obsolete organization Publish Logo is prohibited.
- Keep the Logo clear of cards, borders, and bright aurora details.
- Prefer the horizontal dark Logo on covers and the emblem for compact navigation or avatars.

## Visual system

- Base canvas: Deep Navy `#091231`.
- Background image: `borealis.avif`, cropped as cover and darkened enough to protect text contrast.
- Headings: `#F0FCFF`; body: `#E0E7EA`.
- Primary accent: Signal Cyan `#29DDDA`; Mint `#16FFBB` and Ice Blue `#37A7E7` are secondary.
- Cards: `rgba(10,18,49,0.78)`, 12 px radius, restrained translucent border, maximum 10 px backdrop blur.
- Use the blue-green gradient only on the M emblem, a single focal line, or one primary data series—not everywhere.

## Typography

- Headings: Spline Sans 700.
- Screen body: Barlow 400/500.
- Long-form quotation or reflective passage: Charter / Source Han Serif SC.
- The artistic Martin wordmark is a Logo, not a heading font.

## Composition

- Preserve strong empty space and one dominant focal point.
- Use glass cards only when they improve grouping; dense reading blocks should sit on an opaque or near-opaque navy surface.
- Use the Balanced Signal curve as a restrained divider or motion path.
- Prefer real photographs, screenshots, diagrams, and personal artifacts over generic imagery.

## Voice

Calm Authority · Evidence-led Insight · Human Curiosity.

Open with a useful tension, move from observation to pattern to implication, and close with clarity. Avoid exaggerated futurism, motivational slogans, corporate filler, neon cyberpunk clichés, circuit textures, glowing locks, and decorative dashboards.

## Accessibility

- Body text must meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Do not place body copy directly over a bright aurora region.
- Never use cyan or mint for paragraph text.
- Encode chart meaning through labels and shapes as well as color.

## Relationship to Work

Life and Work remain separate. If the artifact represents Organization Brand or company responsibilities, switch to the Work brand and its templates. A Martin byline may remain; the personal Logo does not.
