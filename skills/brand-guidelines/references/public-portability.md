# Public Portability Contract

This public package preserves Martin's personal `Life` identity and the Life-versus-Work decision model while deliberately excluding private organization names, internal Logos, customer context, and workstation-specific paths.

## Work adapter

`Organization Brand` is a placeholder contract, not a real identity. Before using the Work route:

1. Supply an approved organization Brand Guideline and Logo family.
2. Replace `assets/organization-placeholder/organization-logo.svg` with authorized assets.
3. Map the organization palette, typography, endorsement rules, and document templates.
4. Keep Martin's personal Logo subordinate or absent on organization-authorized outputs.

Do not infer, redraw, or publish an organization's identity from private workspace examples.
